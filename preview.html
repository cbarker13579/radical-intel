🔄 Talent Trends
Top researchers will return to Big Tech — if the terms are right. Startup counter-offers just got harder.
Noam Shazeer returned to Google DeepMind as a Distinguished Engineer after Character.AI's acquisition closed. This is the highest-profile signal yet that retention packages at hyperscalers are competitive again at the very top of the market.
→ Newcomer
The talent you need is hiding under the wrong job title — and your search strings are missing them.
Demand for "AI deployment engineers" — a role blending MLOps, solutions architecture, and prompt engineering — is up 3x YoY at Series B+ startups. The title isn't standardized; candidates self-describe as ML engineers, platform engineers, or solutions architects depending on their last company.
→ Pragmatic Engineer
OpenAI's enterprise PM alumni are becoming one of the most sought-after cohorts in vertical AI — start mapping them now.
Three senior PMs from OpenAI's enterprise team departed in the same week, all joining early-stage vertical AI startups. This follows a quieter wave of mid-level exits over the past 90 days.

🛠️ Field Notes
Equity compression is quietly killing your close rate on senior technical hires — and candidates are comparing notes.
Pave's Q1 2026 benchmark shows AI/ML engineer cash comp up 14% YoY at Series B companies, while equity grants shrank ~20% as valuations reset. Refreshes and accelerated vesting milestones are increasingly table stakes in competitive offers.
→ Pave Blog
Outreach response rates are rising when you lead with the problem, not the role — here's the data.
Recruiting Brainfood's latest practitioner survey found that messages framing the company's specific challenge before mentioning the role title outperform standard InMails by 40% in response rate. Passive candidates at AI companies are pitched constantly — relevance is the differentiator.
→ Recruiting Brainfood
